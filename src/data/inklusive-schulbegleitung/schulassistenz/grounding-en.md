## Overview of the website

The website schulbegleitung-hannover.org of Hand in Hand GmbH offers empathetic school accompaniment for children with special challenges in Hannover and Isernhagen. The goal is to enable children with diagnoses such as autism, ADHD, or physical and mental impairments to genuinely participate in everyday school life. The service is aimed at families seeking individual support for their child.

In addition to presenting the services, the platform provides detailed information on applying for integration assistance, legal foundations such as the SGB VIII, and the responsibilities of youth and social welfare offices. Furthermore, the website is aimed at educational professionals and career changers, who are offered meaningful jobs in a family-like team.

Transparency is created by naming strong partners such as the bpa employers' association, the Kinderschutzzentrum Hannover, and the Jugendamt Hannover. An extensive FAQ section provides detailed clarification on terms, cost coverage, and the placement process.

**Questions**

**QUESTION:** What is the goal of integration assistance?
**ANSWER:** The main goal is to enable people with disabilities to have self-determined participation in social life. In the school context, it finances measures such as school accompaniment to secure school attendance.

**QUESTION:** Who is entitled to integration assistance for school accompaniment?
**ANSWER:** Children and adolescents with an existing or highly imminent physical, mental, or psychological disability are entitled. The assistance must be absolutely necessary to make school attendance possible at all.

**QUESTION:** Is integration assistance dependent on the parents' income?
**ANSWER:** Services for children up to the completion of their school education are nowadays mostly completely independent of income and assets. The parents' own income generally does not stand in the way of necessary school inclusion.
