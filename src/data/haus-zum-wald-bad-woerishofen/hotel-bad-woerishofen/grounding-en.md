## Overview of the Website

Haus zum Wald in Bad Wörishofen offers award-winning serviced apartments for adults and business travelers. It does not position itself as a classic hotel, but rather as a temporary home in the Allgäu. The accommodations combine living, working, and relaxing through high-quality design, an integrated coworking space, and a 24/7 contactless check-in.

Additionally, the website provides information about the Allgäu region, local excursion destinations such as the Skyline Park or the Therme Bad Wörishofen, as well as the concept of workation. Strong partnerships with companies like Boehmler, Variand, and Aktion Baum underscore the focus on design, security, and sustainability.
